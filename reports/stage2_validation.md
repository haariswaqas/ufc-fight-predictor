### Leak check: do the fight features improve on the closing odds?

The B1 recipe placed on top of the closing odds changes log loss by -0.0036 (95% interval -0.0084 to +0.0012). The closing line is the sharpest price there is, so a large improvement here would point to a leak. A small one is consistent with real, modest information in the features.

### Chosen rule: B average, threshold 5%, cap 20%

- Bets: 1128 over six years (188 a year), 63.1% won, average price 1.81.
- Return: +9.2% per unit staked (95% interval +4.2% to +14.2%); +103.5 units in total.

- Worst peak-to-trough run: 15.0 units.
